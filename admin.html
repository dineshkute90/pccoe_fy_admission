Meaning of Symbols & Legends

Official reference keys for allotment data symbols and codes

Document Receipts Symbols

$

$ (Dollar sign): Uploaded receipt for Caste / Tribe Validity Certificate.

@

@ (At symbol): Uploaded receipt for EWS (Economically Weaker Section) Certificate.

#

# (Hash symbol): Uploaded receipt for Non-Creamy Layer (NCL) Certificate.

Legends for SeatType

  • H: Home University
  • O: Other than Home University
  • S: State Level
  • G: General | L: Ladies
  • AI: All India
  • MI: Minority Seats (Allotted to Minority Candidates of eligible Religious/Linguistic community)
  • PWDR: PWD Common Reserved Seat
  • DEFR: Defense Common Reserved Seat
  • Merit No: State General Merit No.

Legends for ChoiceCode

  • L: Regional Language
  • F: Female
  • T: Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme (TFWS)
  • U: UnAided
  • K: Konkan
